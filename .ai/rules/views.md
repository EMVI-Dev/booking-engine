# Views

## Everyday language on the welcome page
Marketing copy talks to tour shop owners, not developers. Do not use coding, white-label, or operating-system wording. JSON-LD may keep SoftwareApplication as the type.

## Marketing Operator log in stays on the platform
Operator log in on the marketing site uses platform route('login'). Only Try the operator desk opens demo.{platform}/login (nofollow). Never send a no-slug login button to the demo shop.

## No emoji in product copy or icons
No emoji in product UI, emails, or guest WhatsApp/calendar copy. Web icons are Font Awesome (`fa-solid ...`). Emails use plain text, not emoji prefixes. Select options may pass an `icon` class and optional `hint`.

## Sample shop sits at the bottom
Welcome hero is read-first: only the sign-up / coming-soon CTA. See a sample shop and Try the operator desk live in a simple section after the closing CTA, with rel=noopener nofollow and no wire:navigate. Do not put live demo links in the hero or the mobile product snapshot.

## Call it a platform fee, not a guest burden
On the welcome page, say platform fee — never guest fee, guest pays, or paid by customer. Facts and pricing stay short and operator-first (you keep 100%; a 5% platform fee is added at checkout). Put the fuller explanation in the FAQ. Do not change the admin setting label unless asked.

## Platform fee is not a plan feature
Do not list the 5% platform fee as a plan feature or comparison-table row. It is the same on every plan and is not a perk. Keep it on the pricing cards, the honest note, and the FAQ.

## Plan lists are shipped features only
Welcome plan cards and the compare table list only shipped operator features (Plan flags the product actually uses). No strikethrough wishlist, no commercials, no unshipped flags such as byo_gateway. Each card shows only what that plan includes.

## Pay links are copy-and-share, not WhatsApp API
Do not claim a WhatsApp API. Pay links are copied and pasted by the operator. Say copy, share, or paste in chat — never send in WhatsApp. wa.me click-to-chat is fine to show as an example of where they paste.
