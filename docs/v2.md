# V2 — not in the product yet

Do not seed or show Enterprise until this file says ship.

Who: large operators (several venues, several departures, ground staff).
EMVI stays the booking + ops engine. They keep their own marketing site.

Features stay general. Each company runs the day differently — do not bake one operator's SOP into the product.

## Slices

1. Embeddable booking calendar — a snippet they paste on their own site. Guests pick a date and pay in EMVI. Their marketing site stays theirs.
2. Guest check-in — QR on the e-ticket, scan on a phone, marked on the day's list
3. More than one departure per day, each with its own capacity
4. Extra day-of guest details the operator chooses to collect (pickup, notes, and the like)
5. A limited staff login for people on the ground (check-in only)

## Not V2

No marketing-site rebuild. No marketplace. No BYO gateway.
No assigning boats, piers, or named guides as a built-in workflow.
